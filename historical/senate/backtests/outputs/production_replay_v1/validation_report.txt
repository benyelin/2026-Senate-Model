Senate Historical Production Replay — Version 1
========================================================================

Central model: presidential baseline + 0.90 × generic ballot
Scope: race-level and seats-up simulation; not full-chamber control
Cycles: 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024
Scorable races: 234
Simulations per cycle: 50,000

Overall metrics

Important:
